# FPS-LAN — Playtesting

## Purpose

This project will be developed through real LAN sessions.

After each meaningful milestone, play with the actual target group.

## Questions to Ask

### Combat
- Are encounters happening often enough?
- Does shooting feel satisfying?
- Are weapons readable?
- Is damage feedback clear?
- Are kills too easy or too difficult?

### Movement
- Does movement feel responsive?
- Is sprint useful?
- Is jumping useful?
- Does the player feel too slow or too fast?

### Map
- Are there dead areas?
- Are there too many long sightlines?
- Are players finding each other quickly?
- Are spawns safe?
- Are some areas clearly stronger than others?

### Multiplayer
- Is connection simple?
- Are there visible desync problems?
- Do players appear correctly?
- Does shooting feel consistent across machines?

## Development Rule

Do not add mechanics just because they sound cool.

A new mechanic should have a clear gameplay purpose or emerge from playtesting.

When players report a problem, investigate the root cause before adding a new system.
